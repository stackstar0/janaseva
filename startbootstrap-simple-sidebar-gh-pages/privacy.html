Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2025

This Privacy Policy outlines how Janasava, operated by Corepair, collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our portal for document-related services.

1. Information We Collect
  • Personal Details: Full name, date of birth, gender, mobile number, email address.
  • Identification Documents: Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, passport photo, signatures, and certificates.
  • Device/Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, interaction data (non-personal).
2. How We Use Your Information
  • To process document applications (e.g. PAN, Aadhaar, income/caste certificates).
  • To validate user identity and prevent fraud.
  • To contact users regarding their requests or queries.
  • To improve platform usability and security.
3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
  • We only share your data with government departments or officials strictly for service purposes.
  • We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties.
  • Third-party partners (e.g. file storage or authentication services) must comply with data protection standards.
4. Your Consent

By submitting your information through our portal, you consent to our use and storage of it in accordance with this policy.

5. Data Security
  • We use HTTPS encryption and secure file storage.
  • Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
6. Your Rights
  • You can request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data at any time.
  • To make a request, email us at support@janasava.in
7. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies only for session control and analytics. No tracking cookies are installed for ads or profiling.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as required for application processing or legal compliance. Once complete, non-essential data may be deleted or anonymized.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users above 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about your privacy, you can contact:

  • Operator: Corepair
  • Email: support@janasava.in
